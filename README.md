1) Objective 

The primary objective of this project is to develop a semantic classification model capable of accurately distinguishing between true and fake news articles. This involves leveraging the Word2Vec methodology to extract semantic relations from the text data and applying supervised learning techniques for categorization. The project aims to highlight the importance of understanding textual meaning in critical decision-making scenarios. 

1) Business Objective 

The proliferation of fake news poses a significant threat to the integrity of information in the digital age. The ability to automatically classify news articles as true or fake is crucial for mitigating the spread of misinformation and preserving public trust. This project addresses this need by creating a system that can effectively identify and classify news articles based on their veracity.
